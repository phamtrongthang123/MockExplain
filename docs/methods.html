Suggested methods

Methods currently covered by MockExplain and where to read more.

These are suggestions based on structural clues. MockExplain does not run the methods.

Find sparse features in model activations. Paper ↗

Activation inspection

Inspect activations at layers or heads. TransformerLens ↗

RULES

How suggestions are chosen

  • Convolution clues suggest filter inspection and Grad-CAM.
  • Attention clues suggest SAEs and activation inspection. When embeddings and a possible vocabulary head are also found, J-lens and logit lens replace activation inspection.
  • For convolution-and-attention hybrids, suggestions are limited to filter inspection, Grad-CAM, J-lens when language clues are present, and SAEs.
  • Unknown structures receive no method suggestion.

Clues are not proof of model family or method compatibility. Open Details in the app for each method’s requirements and limits.
